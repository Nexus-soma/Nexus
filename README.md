# Nexus

**Not a chatbot. A companion.**

Nexus is a local-first, cross-device AI agent. It treats your PC and your phone not as separate devices, but as a single body. One intelligence, two hands.

---

## The Vision

Current AI agents are device-bound. Your computer has one. Your phone has another. They don't talk. They don't share context. They don't feel like a unified presence.

Nexus is different.

- It lives on your PC.
- It controls your phone when needed.
- It remembers everything.
- It acts proactively — not just when you ask.
- It can call for help when you can't.
- It's completely local and private.
- No tokens. No cloud. No subscription. Yours.

## Architecture

- **Brain**: Decision-making, memory, tool routing
- **Phone Bridge**: Screen reading, tap/type automation, sensor access
- **Safety Layer**: Confirmation levels, emergency protocols
- **Memory Core**: Short, medium, long-term storage — all local
